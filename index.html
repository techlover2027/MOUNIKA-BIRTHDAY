To Mounika — My Elder Sister ❤️

Happy Birthday to the girl who somehow turned from a stranger into someone I can call my sister. 🥳❤️

Today isn't just another birthday. Maybe it's a small reminder that you've completed another chapter of your life — and now, it's time to start a better one.

When I look back, it's actually funny how our journey started. It's been only around three months since we met, but somehow, within these three months, we've collected so many conversations, memories, laughs, problems, and unexpected moments. And yes… I still remember how I literally installed Snapchat because of you. 😂

From those first conversations to everything that happened after that, our journey has had so many shifts. Some days were full of fun, some were confusing, some were emotional, and some were just completely chaotic. But every part of it became a small piece of our story.

And then came the days when your health wasn't on your side. Getting a blood infection and being hospitalized for three days wasn't easy. Seeing you go through that made me realize something simple — your health comes before everything else.

So please, start taking care of yourself properly. Eat well, don't skip meals, drink enough water, and give your body what it needs. Add fruits and vegetables to your food — things like spinach and other leafy greens, beans, peas, lentils, oranges, guava, apples, and other fresh fruits can be good additions to a balanced diet. And most importantly, follow whatever your doctor has advised you.

You don't always have to be strong for everyone else. Sometimes, being strong simply means taking care of yourself.

And Mounika… I know things haven't been easy lately.

You felt hurt because some friendships changed. You had problems with people, and what happened with Yogitha affected you so much that it brought tears and even started affecting your studies. I know losing or damaging a friendship can hurt more than people realize.

But please don't let one painful chapter convince you that the whole story is over.

If your friendship with Yogitha still means something to you, don't give up on it because of what others did. Give it time. Stay genuine. Don't force anything. Sometimes friendships need space before they find their way back.

And please don't let friendship problems take away the student inside you.

We're both in Intermediate First Year at Vedantu Learning Centers, and this is the time when you've got a path to build. Your studies deserve your attention too. One bad phase doesn't decide your marks, and one difficult month doesn't decide your future.

You've still got time.

A Small Promise To Yourself 🤞
  • Don't let people decide your mood.
  • Don't let one friendship decide your worth.
  • Don't let one bad phase decide your future.

And please don't go into that "full silent mode" you were talking about. 😭

You're one of the most talkative people I've known, and suddenly deciding that you're going to stop talking to everyone and isolate yourself isn't the answer.

You don't have to talk to everyone.
You don't have to trust everyone.
You don't have to explain yourself to everyone.

But don't shut the whole world out just because a few people hurt you.

Stay neutral. Be kind. Keep your distance from unnecessary drama. Talk to the people who genuinely care about you.

And if something is bothering you, you can tell me.

You don't have to keep everything inside and pretend you're completely fine. Whether it's something serious, something stupid, something about friendship, studies, or just a random bad day — say it. Sometimes sharing what's on your mind makes things a little lighter.

This Birthday Is Your Reset Button ✨

Not because you have to suddenly become a completely different person. But because from today, you can start becoming a better version of yourself.

  • ✨ Wake up with a little more purpose.
  • ✨ Study with a little more focus.
  • ✨ Take care of your health.
  • ✨ Protect your peace.
  • ✨ Keep the people who genuinely matter.
  • ✨ Let go of unnecessary drama.

And slowly, step by step, start building the path you want for yourself. You don't need to fix everything in one day. Just start.

Because sometimes the biggest changes begin with one ordinary decision:
"Okay… from today, I'll try again."

So Mounika, on your birthday, I hope you smile more, worry less, eat properly, study better, take care of yourself, and never forget that difficult phases don't last forever.

You've already come through a lot in these past few months.

Now it's your turn to write the next part.

• No more letting bad days control you.
• No more losing yourself because of people.
• No more giving up on yourself.

This is your day.
Your reset. Your comeback. Your next chapter. ❤️

And maybe, three months ago, I never imagined that installing Snapchat for a girl would eventually lead to me writing a whole birthday letter for my elder sister.

Life really has its own funny way of creating stories. 😂❤️

So once again…

Happy Birthday, Mounika Diii! 🥳❤️

Keep that smile. Keep that madness. Keep being you — just a little stronger, a little wiser, and a lot more focused.

Now go make this year yours.
You've got a path to build.
Go walk it. ✨
